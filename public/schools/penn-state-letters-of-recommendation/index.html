Does Penn State Require Letters of Recommendation?

Penn State does not require letters of recommendation for standard freshman admission but accepts one optional letter. Applicants may submit a recommendation from a teacher, counselor, or employer if they feel it adds meaningful information.

What Penn State Uses Instead of Recommendation Letters

Because Penn State does not require letters of recommendation, the application relies primarily on other materials to evaluate candidates. These typically include high school transcripts, course rigor and grades, standardized test scores if submitted, and personal essays or short-answer responses. Some applicants find this format more straightforward, while others prefer the opportunity to have a recommender add outside context. The essays, in particular, carry significant weight in the absence of letters.

Scholarship and Honors Program Exceptions

While Penn State's standard admission process does not require letters, scholarship programs, honors colleges, and specific major tracks may have different requirements. If you are applying to any of these programs at Penn State, review their separate application materials carefully, as letters of recommendation may be required or strongly recommended.

Verify current requirements: Admission requirements can change from year to year. Always confirm letter of recommendation policies directly on Penn State University's official admissions website before completing your application.

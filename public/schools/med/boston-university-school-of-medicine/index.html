Boston University Chobanian and Avedisian School of Medicine Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Boston University Chobanian and Avedisian School of Medicine evaluates letters of recommendation as evidence of academic achievement, service to community, and personal character. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

BU prefers a committee letter from applicants whose schools offer pre-medical advising committees. Applicants without committee support should submit at least three individual letters including two science faculty letters and one letter from a clinical or service supervisor. Up to six letters are accepted through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

Letters from faculty who supervised research or taught advanced science courses, combined with letters from clinical or community health supervisors, form the most useful packages for BU. Given BU's primary care and community health focus, letters from mentors who observed the applicant working with underserved or diverse populations are particularly relevant.

How to Submit Letters to Boston University Chobanian and Avedisian School of Medicine

Letters are submitted through AMCAS. Review BU's secondary application for any updated guidance.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

BU values commitment to community health and social determinants of medicine. Letters that describe the applicant's work in free clinics, community health organizations, or underserved settings add relevant context to the file.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Boston University Chobanian and Avedisian School of Medicine's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Boston University Chobanian and Avedisian School of Medicine Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Boston University Chobanian and Avedisian School of Medicine require?

Boston University Chobanian and Avedisian School of Medicine typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter preferred; individual letters accepted.

Does Boston University Chobanian and Avedisian School of Medicine require a committee letter?

Committee letter preferred; individual letters accepted. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Boston University Chobanian and Avedisian School of Medicine?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Boston University Chobanian and Avedisian School of Medicine?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai evaluates letters of recommendation as part of a holistic review process that values research, clinical commitment, and diversity of background. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

Mount Sinai accepts committee letters and individual letters. A typical package for MD applicants includes two science faculty letters and one or two letters from clinical, research, or service supervisors. Up to six letters are accepted through AMCAS. FlexMed applicants should review program-specific letter requirements separately.

Who Should Write Your Letters

For the standard MD program, science faculty and research mentors are appropriate letter writers. For applicants in non-traditional pathways, faculty from any discipline who can speak to analytical thinking, intellectual rigor, and problem-solving are valuable. Clinical or service supervisors who can describe patient interaction and motivation round out a strong package.

How to Submit Letters to Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai

Letters are submitted through AMCAS. Mount Sinai's secondary application may include specific guidance for different programs.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

Mount Sinai is known for its research output and diverse patient population. Letters that connect the applicant's background to serving diverse urban communities or contributing to scientific discovery align well with the school's culture.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai require?

Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter accepted; individual letters acceptable.

Does Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai require a committee letter?

Committee letter accepted; individual letters acceptable. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

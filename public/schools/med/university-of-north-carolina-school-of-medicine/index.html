University of North Carolina School of Medicine Letters of Recommendation Requirements

UNC School of Medicine has a strong commitment to primary care, rural health, and serving North Carolina communities. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

UNC prefers a committee letter. Applicants without committee access should submit at least three individual letters including two science faculty letters and one from a clinical or service supervisor. Up to six letters are accepted through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

Given UNC's primary care and rural health mission, letters from supervisors who have observed the applicant working with underserved communities, rural health programs, or community health organizations are particularly relevant. Faculty letters and research mentor letters provide the academic dimension.

How to Submit Letters to University of North Carolina School of Medicine

Letters are submitted through AMCAS. Review UNC's secondary application each cycle for any updates, particularly regarding in-state applicant requirements.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

UNC strongly prioritizes North Carolina residents and applicants with ties to the state's health needs. Letters that connect the applicant's background to serving North Carolina's diverse communities add meaningful context.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on University of North Carolina School of Medicine's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

University of North Carolina School of Medicine Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does University of North Carolina School of Medicine require?

University of North Carolina School of Medicine typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter preferred; individual letters accepted.

Does University of North Carolina School of Medicine require a committee letter?

Committee letter preferred; individual letters accepted. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for University of North Carolina School of Medicine?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to University of North Carolina School of Medicine?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

Georgetown University School of Medicine Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Georgetown University School of Medicine, guided by its Jesuit mission, considers letters of recommendation as evidence of character, service orientation, and intellectual formation in addition to academic ability. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

Georgetown prefers a committee letter. Applicants without committee support should submit at least three individual letters including two science faculty letters and one letter from a person who can speak to the applicant's service, community engagement, or clinical experience. Up to six letters are typically accepted through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

Given Georgetown's mission emphasis, letters from people who have supervised the applicant in service roles, volunteer work, or community health settings are particularly relevant. Science faculty letters and research mentor letters remain important, but a letter from someone who can speak to the applicant's values and motivation for medicine adds distinctive weight.

How to Submit Letters to Georgetown University School of Medicine

Letters are submitted through AMCAS. Review the Georgetown secondary application each cycle for updated guidance.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

Georgetown's mission of cura personalis (care for the whole person) shapes its admissions culture. Letters that describe an applicant's genuine curiosity about others, service leadership, or commitment to underserved communities speak directly to what Georgetown looks for.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Georgetown University School of Medicine's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Georgetown University School of Medicine Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Georgetown University School of Medicine require?

Georgetown University School of Medicine typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter preferred; individual letters required if no committee.

Does Georgetown University School of Medicine require a committee letter?

Committee letter preferred; individual letters required if no committee. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Georgetown University School of Medicine?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Georgetown University School of Medicine?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

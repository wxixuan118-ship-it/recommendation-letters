Yale School of Medicine Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Yale School of Medicine evaluates letters of recommendation for evidence of intellectual ability, personal integrity, clinical aptitude, and commitment to medicine as a career of service. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

Yale strongly prefers a committee letter from the applicant's pre-medical advisory office. Applicants without committee support should submit at least three individual letters. A typical letter package includes two letters from science faculty and at least one from a non-academic supervisor or mentor. Yale generally accepts up to six letters through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

Letters from science faculty who supervised research or taught courses where the applicant demonstrated analytical ability are especially useful. Letters from physicians, service coordinators, or clinical supervisors can speak to empathy, patient communication, and commitment to care. Yale values letters from recommenders who can describe the applicant's character beyond the transcript.

How to Submit Letters to Yale School of Medicine

Letters are submitted through the AMCAS Letter Service. Check the Yale secondary application each cycle for any updates to letter requirements or instructions for specific programs.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

Yale's curriculum emphasizes independent inquiry and collaborative learning. If your recommenders can speak to intellectual curiosity, self-directed learning, or research initiative, those themes align well with Yale's academic culture. Provide recommenders with specific examples of your best work together.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Yale School of Medicine's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Yale School of Medicine Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Yale School of Medicine require?

Yale School of Medicine typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter strongly preferred.

Does Yale School of Medicine require a committee letter?

Committee letter strongly preferred. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Yale School of Medicine?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Yale School of Medicine?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

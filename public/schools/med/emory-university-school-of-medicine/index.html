Emory University School of Medicine Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Emory University School of Medicine reviews letters of recommendation alongside the full application file to understand the applicant's academic preparation, clinical exposure, research background, and personal character. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

Emory accepts committee letters and individual letters. Most applicants submit three to five letters including two science faculty letters and one or two letters from clinical, research, or community supervisors. Up to six letters are generally accepted through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

Science faculty who can speak to intellectual rigor and research potential, combined with clinical or service supervisors who have observed patient interaction and professionalism, form the most effective letter packages for Emory. Letters from people who have worked with the applicant over at least one semester or project cycle are more credible than brief acquaintance letters.

How to Submit Letters to Emory University School of Medicine

Letters are submitted through AMCAS. Review Emory's secondary application each cycle for any updated requirements.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

Emory has a strong public health and global health focus. If your experience includes work with underserved communities, global health programs, or public health research, ensure at least one recommender can speak to that dimension of your background.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Emory University School of Medicine's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Emory University School of Medicine Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Emory University School of Medicine require?

Emory University School of Medicine typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter accepted; individual letters acceptable.

Does Emory University School of Medicine require a committee letter?

Committee letter accepted; individual letters acceptable. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Emory University School of Medicine?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Emory University School of Medicine?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

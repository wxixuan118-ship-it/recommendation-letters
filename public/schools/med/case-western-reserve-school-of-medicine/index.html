Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine evaluates letters of recommendation as part of its holistic admissions process. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

Case Western prefers a committee letter. Applicants without committee access should submit at least three individual letters including two science faculty letters and one from a clinical, research, or service supervisor. Up to six letters are accepted through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

Faculty who supervised research or taught the applicant in upper-level science courses are well-positioned to write effective letters. Clinical or service supervisors who can describe patient interaction and communication add important balance. Given CWRU's collaborative culture, letters that describe the applicant's ability to work in teams or mentor others can be particularly effective.

How to Submit Letters to Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine

Letters are submitted through AMCAS. Review Case Western's secondary application for updated requirements.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

Case Western's University Program (integrated with undergraduate studies) and its research-heavy curriculum reward applicants who can demonstrate early research and clinical exposure. Letters that describe longitudinal involvement in research or clinical environments, rather than brief shadowing, carry more weight.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine require?

Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter preferred; individual letters accepted.

Does Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine require a committee letter?

Committee letter preferred; individual letters accepted. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

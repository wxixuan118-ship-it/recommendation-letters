Ohio State University College of Medicine Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Ohio State University College of Medicine combines a large research enterprise with a commitment to primary care and community health. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

Ohio State accepts committee letters and individual letters. Most competitive applicants submit three to five letters including two science faculty letters and at least one from a clinical or service supervisor. Up to six letters are accepted through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

Faculty who supervised research at OSU or affiliated programs are positioned to write effective letters. Clinical supervisors, community health mentors, and service leaders who observed patient-focused work add the service dimension. Letters should describe direct observations of performance, not just general character.

How to Submit Letters to Ohio State University College of Medicine

Letters are submitted through AMCAS. Review OSU's secondary application each cycle for updated requirements.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

OSU's Wexner Medical Center is a major academic medical system. Letters from supervisors who observed the applicant in Wexner clinical environments, research labs, or affiliated programs add relevant context.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Ohio State University College of Medicine's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Ohio State University College of Medicine Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Ohio State University College of Medicine require?

Ohio State University College of Medicine typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter preferred; individual letters accepted.

Does Ohio State University College of Medicine require a committee letter?

Committee letter preferred; individual letters accepted. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Ohio State University College of Medicine?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Ohio State University College of Medicine?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

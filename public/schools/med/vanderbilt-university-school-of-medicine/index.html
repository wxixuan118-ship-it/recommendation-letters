Vanderbilt University School of Medicine Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Vanderbilt University School of Medicine uses letters of recommendation to evaluate leadership, intellectual curiosity, and commitment to service alongside academic performance. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

Vanderbilt accepts committee letters and individual letters. Most competitive applicants submit three to five letters, including two science faculty letters and at least one letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor. Up to six letters are typically accepted through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

Faculty who have supervised research, mentored independent projects, or taught the applicant in demanding coursework are well positioned to write useful letters. Clinical supervisors, service leaders, or community mentors who can describe the applicant's character and motivation add important perspective.

How to Submit Letters to Vanderbilt University School of Medicine

All letters are submitted through AMCAS. Check the Vanderbilt secondary application for any updated requirements each admissions cycle.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

Vanderbilt's culture emphasizes collaboration and community engagement. Letters from recommenders who can speak to the applicant's work with underserved populations, leadership in student organizations, or commitment to health equity can complement the academic letters well.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Vanderbilt University School of Medicine's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Vanderbilt University School of Medicine Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Vanderbilt University School of Medicine require?

Vanderbilt University School of Medicine typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter accepted; individual letters acceptable.

Does Vanderbilt University School of Medicine require a committee letter?

Committee letter accepted; individual letters acceptable. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Vanderbilt University School of Medicine?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Vanderbilt University School of Medicine?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

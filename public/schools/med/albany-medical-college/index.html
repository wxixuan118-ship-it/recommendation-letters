Albany Medical College Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Albany Medical College reviews letters of recommendation as part of its holistic admissions process. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

Most competitive applicants submit between three and six letters. A typical package includes two letters from science faculty who taught or supervised the applicant in a laboratory or classroom setting, and at least one letter from a non-science recommender such as a physician, service supervisor, research mentor, or academic advisor. Albany accepts committee letters from pre-medical advisory offices but does not require them.

Who Should Write Your Letters

The strongest letters come from people who know the applicant well and can describe specific observations. A science professor who graded papers, supervised lab work, or held regular office hours with the applicant can speak to academic rigor. A physician, volunteer supervisor, or clinical mentor can speak to service motivation, communication, and professionalism. Research mentors are especially valuable for applicants who completed significant research.

How to Submit Letters to Albany Medical College

All letters are submitted through the AMCAS Letter Service. Applicants assign letters to individual school applications after letters are uploaded. Letter authors receive a unique link to upload directly. AMCAS does not forward letters with incomplete applications, so all letters should be uploaded before or shortly after the primary application is verified.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

Ask recommenders early, ideally three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, a copy of your activities list, a transcript, and two or three specific examples of work you completed together. A well-informed recommender writes a more specific and credible letter. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Albany Medical College's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Albany Medical College Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Albany Medical College require?

Albany Medical College typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter accepted, not required.

Does Albany Medical College require a committee letter?

Committee letter accepted, not required. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Albany Medical College?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Albany Medical College?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

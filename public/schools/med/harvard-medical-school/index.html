Harvard Medical School Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Harvard Medical School treats letters of recommendation as a critical part of holistic review. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

HMS strongly prefers a committee letter from a pre-medical advisory committee. If the applicant's undergraduate institution does not have a committee, three or more individual letters are required. A typical package includes two science faculty letters and at least one letter from a supervisor who can speak to non-academic qualities such as service, empathy, or leadership. HMS typically accepts up to six letters through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

The most useful letters come from faculty who taught the applicant in upper-level science courses or supervised independent research, and from mentors who observed the applicant in clinical, service, or leadership settings. Letters that describe specific observations, concrete examples, and meaningful comparisons to other applicants carry more weight than letters focused on grades alone.

How to Submit Letters to Harvard Medical School

Letters are submitted through the AMCAS Letter Service. The Harvard secondary application may include specific instructions about letter types or the committee letter requirement. Review those instructions carefully each cycle because requirements can change.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

For applicants applying from institutions with pre-medical committees, begin the committee letter process well before the application cycle opens. Committee letters often require their own interviews, essays, and transcripts. Individual recommenders should be given at least six to eight weeks to write a thoughtful letter.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Harvard Medical School's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Harvard Medical School Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Harvard Medical School require?

Harvard Medical School typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Pre-med committee letter strongly preferred; individual letters accepted if no committee available.

Does Harvard Medical School require a committee letter?

Pre-med committee letter strongly preferred; individual letters accepted if no committee available. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Harvard Medical School?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Harvard Medical School?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

Duke University School of Medicine Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Duke University School of Medicine uses letters of recommendation as part of a comprehensive review process that evaluates academic excellence, research potential, clinical aptitude, and character. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

Duke strongly prefers a committee letter. Applicants without access to a committee must submit at least three individual letters, typically including two science faculty letters and one letter from a clinical, research, or community supervisor. Duke accepts up to six letters through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

For Duke, letters from research mentors who can speak to the applicant's productivity, intellectual independence, and scientific thinking can be especially persuasive given the research-intensive curriculum. Clinical letters that describe patient communication and empathy round out the package. A letter from a science faculty member who knows the applicant beyond the classroom setting adds credibility.

How to Submit Letters to Duke University School of Medicine

Letters are submitted through the AMCAS Letter Service and are assigned to individual school applications by the applicant. Review the Duke secondary application each cycle for updates.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

Duke's third-year research year makes research experience particularly relevant. If you have significant research experience, make sure your research mentor can describe your project's scope, your intellectual contributions, and your ability to work independently.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Duke University School of Medicine's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Duke University School of Medicine Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Duke University School of Medicine require?

Duke University School of Medicine typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter strongly preferred; three individual letters required if no committee available.

Does Duke University School of Medicine require a committee letter?

Committee letter strongly preferred; three individual letters required if no committee available. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Duke University School of Medicine?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Duke University School of Medicine?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

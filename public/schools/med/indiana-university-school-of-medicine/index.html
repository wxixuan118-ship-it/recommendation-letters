Indiana University School of Medicine Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Indiana University School of Medicine is one of the largest medical schools in the United States and has a strong primary care, rural health, and community medicine mission. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

IU School of Medicine accepts committee letters and individual letters. A typical package includes two science faculty letters and one or two letters from clinical, service, or research supervisors. Up to six letters are accepted through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

Given IU's primary care mission, letters from clinical supervisors who observed patient care in community settings, rural health programs, or underserved environments are particularly relevant. Science faculty and research mentors provide the academic perspective.

How to Submit Letters to Indiana University School of Medicine

Letters are submitted through AMCAS. IU has multiple campuses across Indiana, so review the secondary application for campus-specific guidance.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

IU strongly values applicants with Indiana roots or demonstrated interest in serving Indiana communities. Letters that speak to the applicant's connection to the state or commitment to primary care add meaningful context.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Indiana University School of Medicine's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Indiana University School of Medicine Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Indiana University School of Medicine require?

Indiana University School of Medicine typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter preferred; individual letters accepted.

Does Indiana University School of Medicine require a committee letter?

Committee letter preferred; individual letters accepted. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Indiana University School of Medicine?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Indiana University School of Medicine?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

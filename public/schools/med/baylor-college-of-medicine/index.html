Baylor College of Medicine Letters of Recommendation Requirements

Baylor College of Medicine uses letters of recommendation as part of a holistic review that emphasizes academic excellence, research productivity, and service commitment. This guide covers how many letters are required, who should write them, and how to submit through AMCAS.

Letter of Recommendation Requirements

Baylor prefers a committee letter and requires at least three letters if no committee is available. A typical package includes two science faculty letters and one letter from a research or clinical supervisor. Baylor accepts up to six letters through AMCAS.

Who Should Write Your Letters

Research mentors who can speak to the applicant's scientific output and intellectual initiative are especially valuable at BCM. Clinical supervisors who observed patient care, service leaders, and community health mentors also add important perspective. Letters should describe specific observations rather than general praise.

How to Submit Letters to Baylor College of Medicine

Letters are submitted through AMCAS. Review BCM's secondary application each cycle for updated guidance.

Tips for a Strong Letter Package

BCM has a strong research tradition and Texas Medical Center affiliation. Letters from faculty at BCM-affiliated programs or anyone familiar with the Houston medical environment can add useful context.

Verify current requirements: Medical school admission requirements change by cycle. Always confirm letter requirements directly on Baylor College of Medicine's official admissions website and through the current AMCAS instructions before submitting your application.

Common questions

Baylor College of Medicine Recommendation Letter FAQ

How many letters of recommendation does Baylor College of Medicine require?

Baylor College of Medicine typically requires a minimum of 3 letters and accepts up to 6 letters through AMCAS. Committee letter preferred; three individual letters required without committee.

Does Baylor College of Medicine require a committee letter?

Committee letter preferred; three individual letters required without committee. If your undergraduate school does not have a pre-medical advisory committee, submit the required number of individual letters instead.

Who should write my letters for Baylor College of Medicine?

Most applicants submit 2 letters from science faculty and at least 1 letter from a clinical, research, or service supervisor who can speak to qualities beyond academic performance.

How do I submit letters to Baylor College of Medicine?

Submit through AMCAS Letter Service. Recommenders upload letters through a unique link provided by AMCAS, and applicants then assign letters to each school application.

When should I ask for letters of recommendation?

Ask recommenders three to four months before the application opens. Provide each recommender with your personal statement draft, activities list, transcript, and a few reminders of work you completed together. Follow up with a thank-you after submission.

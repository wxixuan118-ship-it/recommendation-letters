Writing guide

How to use a landlord letter of recommendation template

A landlord letter of recommendation template is an essential tool for property managers and landlords who need to provide rental references for current or former tenants. When a tenant applies for a new rental property, mortgage, or even certain jobs, they often need a letter from their current landlord confirming their rental history. Having a professional template ready saves time and ensures that all the important information is included in a clear, organized format.

The most important elements of a landlord letter of recommendation are the tenancy dates, the property address, the monthly rent amount, and whether the tenant consistently paid rent on time. Property management companies and new landlords rely on this information to assess whether an applicant will be a reliable tenant. According to the National Multifamily Housing Council, rental references are among the top factors landlords consider when evaluating new tenant applications.

Beyond basic rent payment information, a strong landlord recommendation letter can also address the tenant’s overall behavior and responsibility as a renter. Did they keep the property clean and well-maintained? Did they give proper notice before moving? Did they respect neighbors and community rules? Were they responsive to maintenance requests? These details help the next landlord form a complete picture of what kind of tenant the applicant will be.

When writing a landlord letter of recommendation, it is important to stick to facts and avoid personal opinions that could be considered discriminatory. Fair Housing Act guidelines prohibit landlords from making statements about a tenant’s race, religion, national origin, familial status, disability, or other protected characteristics. Focus instead on verifiable facts: payment history, lease compliance, property condition, and communication.

The tone of the letter should be professional and factual. Start with the basic tenancy information, then add details about the tenant’s reliability and responsibility. If there were any issues, be honest but fair. If the tenant was excellent, say so specifically. Close with a clear statement of whether you would rent to them again, and offer to answer any follow-up questions. Include your full name, title, company name if applicable, and contact information.

For tenants requesting a landlord reference, it is a good idea to give your landlord plenty of notice before you need the letter. Provide any specific details or format the new landlord requires. If you had a positive rental history, mention a few highlights to remind your landlord, such as always paying rent early or being a quiet, responsible neighbor. Be polite and follow up if you have not heard back after a few days.

A landlord letter of recommendation template should be adaptable for different situations. Some new landlords simply want confirmation of tenancy and rent payment history. Others want a more detailed character reference. Having a template that can be easily customized with additional paragraphs or details makes the process much faster and ensures consistency across all reference letters you provide as a landlord or property manager.

For property managers who handle multiple rental references, having a standardized landlord letter of recommendation template process can save significant time. Consider creating both a short version for basic verification and a longer version for detailed recommendations, so you can quickly provide the right level of detail for each request. Keep records of all reference letters you provide, including the date, the tenant’s name, and what information was shared, in case questions arise later.

Tenants should also understand that not all landlord references will be glowing. If you had some late payments or other issues during your tenancy, do not panic. Be upfront with potential new landlords about what happened and what you have learned from the experience. A honest explanation paired with evidence of improved behavior can often be more persuasive than a perfect rental history from someone who has never faced challenges.

Final review checklist

  • Tenancy dates and property address are accurate and complete.
  • Rent payment history is clearly stated (on-time, late pattern, etc.).
  • Lease compliance and property condition are mentioned.
  • The tone is professional and factual, avoiding protected characteristics.
  • Landlord contact information and signature are included.

Frequently asked questions

What is a landlord letter of recommendation?

A landlord letter of recommendation is a written reference from a current or former landlord confirming a tenant’s rental history, payment record, and overall reliability as a tenant.

Why do I need a landlord recommendation letter?

New landlords and property managers use landlord recommendation letters to verify rental history and assess whether an applicant will be a reliable, responsible tenant who pays rent on time and takes good care of the property.

Can a landlord refuse to write a recommendation letter?

Yes, landlords are not legally required to provide a recommendation letter. However, most will provide a basic factual confirmation of tenancy and rent payment history upon request.

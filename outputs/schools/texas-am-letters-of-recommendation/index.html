Does Texas A&M Require Letters of Recommendation?

Texas A&M does not require letters of recommendation for standard freshman admission through ApplyTexas. The application focuses on grades, test scores, and essays. Some competitive programs and engineering tracks may have different requirements.

What Texas A&M Uses Instead of Recommendation Letters

Because Texas A&M does not require letters of recommendation, the application relies primarily on other materials to evaluate candidates. These typically include high school transcripts, course rigor and grades, standardized test scores if submitted, and personal essays or short-answer responses. Some applicants find this format more straightforward, while others prefer the opportunity to have a recommender add outside context. The essays, in particular, carry significant weight in the absence of letters.

Scholarship and Honors Program Exceptions

While Texas A&M's standard admission process does not require letters, scholarship programs, honors colleges, and specific major tracks may have different requirements. If you are applying to any of these programs at Texas A&M, review their separate application materials carefully, as letters of recommendation may be required or strongly recommended.

Verify current requirements: Admission requirements can change from year to year. Always confirm letter of recommendation policies directly on Texas A&M University's official admissions website before completing your application.

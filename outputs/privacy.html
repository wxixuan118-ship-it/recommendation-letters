Privacy

Privacy Policy

Recommendation Letters provides editable letter of recommendation templates. This policy explains what information may be collected when users browse templates or submit the download form.

Information We Collect

When a user requests a template download, we may collect the name, email address, use case, selected template, selected file format, and submission time. This information is used to unlock downloads, understand template demand, and send relevant template updates.

We do not ask users to submit passwords through the template download form. If account authentication is added in the future, it should be handled through a dedicated authentication provider rather than a plain contact form.

How We Use Information

Contact information may be used to provide access to downloadable templates, improve the template library, respond to user requests, and understand which recommendation letter categories are most useful. We do not sell personal contact information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site owner through the email address associated with this website's Netlify account or domain registration.
